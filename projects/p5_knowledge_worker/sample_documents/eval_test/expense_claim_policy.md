# Meghna Expense Claim Policy

> Synthetic retrieval-test document. Meghna Cooperative and all rules, codes, and figures in this policy are fictional.

Policy revision: `DOC-EXP-2027-01`. Owner: Finance and Accounts.

## 1. Who can claim

Permanent staff, contract staff and approved volunteers can claim business expenses they paid from their own pocket. Interns can claim only local transport, and only when their supervisor booked the trip. Expenses for spouses, children or friends travelling along are never reimbursed, even on the same trip.

## 2. Submitting a claim

Claims are submitted in the Meghna Finance app within 30 calendar days of the date on the receipt. A claim older than 30 days needs written approval from the Head of Finance and is paid in the next quarter, not the next salary run. Every line on a claim needs a category code, for example `EXP-MEAL` for meals, `EXP-TAXI` for local taxis and ride-hailing, and `EXP-PRINT` for printing and stationery.

## 3. Receipts

A clear photo of the original receipt must be attached to every expense above 500 taka. Below 500 taka a short written note is enough. Handwritten receipts from small shops are accepted only if they show the shop name, the date and a phone number. If a receipt is lost, the employee fills in the lost receipt declaration, form `EXP-LRD`, which can be used at most twice per calendar year.

## 4. Meal limits

When working outside the office for more than 6 hours, an employee can claim up to 350 taka for lunch and up to 450 taka for dinner. Breakfast is reimbursed only when work starts before 7:00 in the morning, up to 200 taka. Meals with external guests need the guests' names and organisation on the claim, with a limit of 1,200 taka per person.

## 5. Local transport

Ride-hailing cars and CNG auto-rickshaws can be claimed for trips inside the city. Motorbike ride-hailing is allowed for trips under 10 kilometres. Taxis to and from the airport are reimbursed in full. Travelling between home and the regular office is a personal cost and cannot be claimed, including on public holidays.

## 6. Approval and payment

The line manager approves claims up to 25,000 taka. Claims between 25,000 and 100,000 taka also need the department head, and anything above 100,000 taka needs the Head of Finance. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary if they are approved by the 20th of the month; claims approved after the 20th are paid one month later.

## 7. Misuse

Submitting the same receipt twice, changing amounts on a receipt or claiming personal costs is treated as misconduct. Finance audits a random 10 percent of claims every quarter. Money paid out for an invalid claim is recovered from the next salary.
