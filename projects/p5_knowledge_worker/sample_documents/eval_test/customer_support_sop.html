1. Support channels

Members can reach support by phone on 09610-123456, by email at help@meghna.example, through live chat in the Meghna app, and at any branch counter. Phone and live chat are staffed from 9:00 to 21:00 every day except the two Eid holidays. Email is answered seven days a week.

2. Ticket priorities and response times

Every request is logged as a ticket and given a priority. The first response time is measured from when the ticket is created.

3. Verifying the caller

Before discussing any account, the agent confirms the member's full name, the last four digits of the account number and the date of birth. Agents must never ask for a PIN, a password or a one-time code. If the caller fails verification twice, the agent ends the call politely and tags the ticket SEC-FAILVER.

4. Escalation

An agent escalates a ticket to a team lead when the resolution target is about to be missed, when the member asks for a supervisor, or when the amount involved is above 50,000 taka. Fraud reports are escalated straight to the Fraud Desk with the tag ESC-FRD, without trying to solve them first. Team leads must pick up an escalated ticket within 30 minutes.

5. Refunds and reversals

Agents can approve a reversal of up to 5,000 taka on their own for a duplicate or failed transaction. Reversals between 5,000 and 50,000 taka need team lead approval, and larger amounts go to Finance. The money reaches the member's account within 3 working days of approval. A member can ask for a refund of a service fee once every 6 months.

6. Closing a ticket

A ticket is closed only after the member confirms that the problem is solved, or after 3 reminder messages over 7 days get no reply. After closing, the member receives a one-question satisfaction survey by SMS. A ticket can be reopened within 14 days of closing; after that a new ticket is created.
