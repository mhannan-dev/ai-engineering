Northstar Office FAQ

Synthetic retrieval-test document. Northstar and all details below are fictional.

Where is the head office?
The head office is on the 9th floor of Meghna Tower, Gulshan Avenue, Dhaka. Reception is open from 08:30 to 19:00, Sunday to Thursday.

How do I book a meeting room?
Meeting rooms are booked through the calendar app. Rooms on the 9th floor are named after rivers: Padma seats 12, Jamuna seats 8, and Karnaphuli seats 4. A booking that nobody checks into within 10 minutes is released automatically.

Is there a canteen?
Yes. The canteen on the 8th floor serves lunch from 12:30 to 14:30. Lunch is subsidised and costs 120 taka per meal, deducted from salary at the end of the month. Tea and coffee are free all day.

What is the Wi-Fi network?
Employees connect to the network named NS-Staff using their company account. Visitors use NS-Guest; reception gives out a voucher that is valid for 8 hours.

How do I get an ID card?
New joiners get a photo ID card from the Facilities desk on their first day. A lost card must be reported to Facilities the same day. A replacement card costs 500 taka.

Is there parking?
There are 20 car parking spaces in the basement, allocated by seniority and need. Motorcycle parking is free and first come, first served. Bicycle racks are available near the main gate.

Who do I contact for a broken chair or air conditioner?
Raise a Facilities ticket with the category FAC-REQ. Urgent issues such as a water leak can also be reported by phone at extension 2200.

Can I work from another Northstar office?
Yes. Hot desks are available at the Chattogram office (Agrabad) and the Sylhet office (Zindabazar). Book a desk at least one working day ahead through the desk booking page.
