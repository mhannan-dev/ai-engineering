# Northstar Leave and Attendance Policy

> Synthetic retrieval-test document. Northstar and all policies, codes, dates, and figures in this policy are fictional.

Policy revision: `DOC-HRM-2026-03`. Owner: People Operations.

## 1. Annual leave

Every full-time employee receives 20 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 1.67 days per completed month, so an employee who joins on 1 July has 10 days available by the end of December. Annual leave is requested in the HR portal under the code `LV-ANN`. Requests of five or more consecutive days must be submitted at least 15 calendar days in advance so the team lead can plan cover.

## 2. Carry-forward and encashment

Up to 10 unused annual leave days may be carried into the next year. Carried days expire on 31 March if they are not taken. Any balance above 10 days is paid out at the basic daily rate in the January payroll; this payout is called leave encashment and appears on the payslip as `ENC-LV`. Employees on notice period cannot carry leave forward and are paid for their remaining balance in the final settlement.

## 3. Sick leave

Employees receive 14 days of paid sick leave per year, recorded under `LV-SCK`. Sick leave does not carry forward and is not encashed. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than two consecutive working days. For hospitalisation, sick leave can be extended by up to 30 additional days at full pay after review by People Operations.

## 4. Parental leave

The birth parent receives 112 calendar days (16 weeks) of paid maternity leave, split as 56 days before and 56 days after the expected delivery date, or taken entirely after delivery if a doctor advises. The other parent receives 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 60 days of the birth. Adoptive parents of a child under two years old receive the same entitlements, counted from the date of placement.

## 5. Public holidays and festival leave

Northstar observes the government public holiday calendar. In addition, each employee may take 2 floating festival days for a religious or cultural festival not covered by the public calendar. Floating days are recorded under `LV-FST`, must be requested 7 days in advance, and cannot be carried forward.

## 6. Unpaid leave

After annual leave is exhausted, an employee may request up to 30 calendar days of unpaid leave per year for personal reasons. Unpaid leave longer than 30 days needs approval from the department head and People Operations. Health insurance cover continues during unpaid leave, but leave does not accrue during unpaid periods.

## 7. Attendance and late arrival

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. An arrival after 10:30 without notice to the team lead is recorded as a late mark. Three late marks in one calendar month are converted into half a day of annual leave. Remote workers follow the same core hours in their own time zone.

## 8. Approval chain

Leave of one to four days is approved by the team lead. Leave of five days or more is approved by the team lead and then the department head. If an approver does not respond within three working days, the request escalates automatically to People Operations, who decide within two further working days.
