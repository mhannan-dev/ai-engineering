Northstar IT Security Guidelines

1. Passwords

Passwords for Northstar accounts must be at least 14 characters long. Passphrases made of four or more unrelated words are encouraged. Passwords are not rotated on a fixed schedule; they must be changed immediately if a compromise is suspected. Reusing a Northstar password on any external website is a policy violation.

2. Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the HR portal, source control, and the VPN. The approved method is an authenticator app or a hardware security key. SMS codes are allowed only as a temporary fallback for up to 14 days while a replacement device is set up.

3. Laptops and devices

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Operating system security updates must be installed within 7 days of release. Personal phones may access email only through the managed mail app, which can be wiped remotely.

4. Reporting a security incident

Suspected phishing, a lost device, or any unexpected access prompt must be reported within 1 hour to the security desk at extension 4400 or through the ticket category SEC-INC. Do not try to investigate a suspicious attachment yourself and do not forward it to colleagues. A lost laptop is remotely locked as soon as the report is received.

5. Data classification

Northstar data has three classes. Public data may be shared freely. Internal data may be shared with any employee but not outside the company. Confidential data, including salary records and customer contracts, may be shared only with named people and must never be pasted into external AI tools or personal cloud storage.

6. Access reviews

Team leads review who has access to their systems every quarter. Access for a leaver is removed on their last working day by 18:00. A contractor account expires automatically after 90 days unless the sponsoring manager renews it.
